2. ANDROID MANIFEST PERMISSIONS DECLARED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. android.permission.INTERNET
   - Required for Firestore, Firebase Authentication, and real-time syncing.
2. android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS
   - Required on Android 13+ (API 33+) for commute alarms & ride updates.
3. android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
   - Reschedules local commute reminders when phone restarts.

NOTE: All unnecessary permissions (including GPS Location ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION,
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION, and SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM /
USE_EXACT_ALARM) have been removed. Pickup zones are designated by township/plant
workplace zones, requiring zero device location access and ensuring instant
Google Play Store compliance.

Full Description (up to 4000 chars):
TravelBuddy is an internal corporate carpooling and commuter coordination application designed to connect employees traveling between townships, residential zones, and workplace facilities.

Key Features:
• Corporate Identity & Approval Gate: Secure onboarding restricted to registered corporate employees with administrator review and verification.
• Daily Commute Scheduling: Automatic and customizable morning/evening commute reminders aligned with company working shifts.
• Live Journey & Vehicle Coordination: Real-time driver seat management, passenger requests, and pickup zone clustering.
• Environmental Sustainability: Live tracking of vehicle trips avoided, individual and collective CO₂ reduction metrics, and management reporting.
• Role Flexibility: Effortlessly switch between Passenger and Driver modes depending on daily availability.
• Privacy & Safety First: Workplace designated zones protect user privacy; no background GPS tracking or device location access required.
